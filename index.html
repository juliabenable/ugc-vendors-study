minisocial, soona (formerly Trend.io) and Insense: price and how managed

We demoed all three for Benable's Q1 content push of 25 to 100 pieces a month. minisocial is fully managed by default. soona and Insense are self-serve tools with a paid managed tier. Their prices count different things, so this page converts each one to cost per piece.

Cost per piece, around 50 pieces

Lowest priceUp to the highest price

Every bar starts at $0. The dark part ends at the lowest price, and the light part runs up to the highest price. soona's range is Level 1 to Level 3. Insense's range is $100 to $200 paid to each creator. Product and shipping are extra for all three. Every number is worked out in the table below.

The deliverables differ. minisocial and Insense influencer posts include the creator posting on their own feed. soona's $89 to $259 buys a licensed video file. Add posting at $500 to $1,000+ per post and even self-serve soona costs about 3 to 5 times minisocial's $220 for the same "creator posts about us" result: ($184 + $500) ÷ $220 = 3.1×, and ($184 + $1,000) ÷ $220 = 5.4×.
